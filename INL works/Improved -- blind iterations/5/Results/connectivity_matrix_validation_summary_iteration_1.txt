Connectivity matrix validation summary - Improved Document 5, Iteration 1

Ground truth normalization:
The GT file was normalized into an undirected edge set. This GT file has source_component and target_component columns. The extracted CSV files were adjacency matrices and were normalized into the same undirected edge-set format. Diego/Jennifer round matrix files and final_connectivity_matrix.csv were evaluated.

GT after normalization: 140 undirected edges across 112 component names.

Main reason the scores were not perfect:
The remaining errors are mostly connectivity/edge-discovery issues, with one small close-name issue. Every evaluated matrix used V-MISV-A/B while the GT uses VLV-MSIV-A/B, which causes two FP and two FN under exact validation. Beyond that, the major score loss comes from extra incorrect edges and missed GT edges, especially GND-GRID-related connections and control/instrumentation edges. Later round and final matrices also omit the GND-GRID node entirely.

Close-but-not-perfect component names:
- V-MISV-A should map to VLV-MSIV-A
- V-MISV-B should map to VLV-MSIV-B

Component coverage:
- Extra extracted component names across the matrices: V-MISV-A, V-MISV-B.
- Missing exact GT component names across the matrices: GND-GRID, VLV-MSIV-A, VLV-MSIV-B.

Examples causing the drop:
- Close-name issue: MCC-1A1 -- V-MISV-A was extracted, while the GT expects MCC-1A1 -- VLV-MSIV-A; same pattern for division B.
- Later round/final matrices are missing GND-GRID, causing missed GT edges such as GND-GRID -- GSU-1, GND-GRID -- LC-480A/B/C/D, and GND-GRID -- TR-RAT-1A/B.
- Repeated false positives include BUS-NON1 -- TR-SAF-C, BUS-SY230 -- TR-UAT-1A/B, CP-ESFAS-1 -- INST-NSSS-B, CP-ESFAS-2 -- INST-NSSS-A, INV-UPS-A/B -- RECT-48V-A/B, JB-RLY-A -- JB-RLY-B, and PNL-DC-A -- PNL-DC-B.
- Repeated missed GT edges include BUS-AUX13B -- BUS-NON1, GEN-G101 -- TR-UAT-1A/B, INST-NSSS-A/B -- UPS-PNL-A/B, and LC-480A/B -- RECT-48V-A/B.
