4) AMBIGUITIES_FOR_JENNIFER
- GND-STA is visible at lower left with a station ground symbol and an unlabeled dashed ground route. I included GND-STA as a standalone node but did not invent an edge to an unnamed route or boundary.
- BUS-120AC-A to RELAY-27A is visually weaker than the train B UV relay tap. The A crop clearly shows RPS-PNL-A dashed to RELAY-27A and labels the relay UV BUS A but the solid bus tap is not as explicit as RELAY-27B.
- BUS-480A to TR-CPT-A and BUS-480A to CHGR-2A share closely spaced vertical auxiliary feeders near the left side of BUS-480A. Both endpoints are visually plausible but the exact branch geometry is crowded.
- BUS-480B to TR-CPT-B and BUS-480B to CHGR-2B use a similar shared right-side auxiliary route. I kept both because the raw and wire overlay show conductors reaching each device.
- RELAY-86A and RELAY-86B are connected by dashed lockout taps from the 125 VDC buses. I did not add further relay-to-RPS or relay-to-station-yard edges because those dashed continuations terminate in tray or boundary geometry without a clear named endpoint.
- The external boxes TO UNIT AUX BUS CONT and TO STATION YARD CONT are readable but I did not include them because no direct conductor endpoint is clearly drawn from those boxes to the circuit.
- Cable identifiers such as 2A-F1 through 2A-F6 and 2B-F1 through 2B-F6 were used only as evidence labels for MCC feeders rather than component nodes.
