AMBIGUITIES
- PT-4160A and PT-4160B are instrument transformer nodes tied to the 4 kV bus by dotted signal geometry rather than a heavy power feeder.
- CB-DC-1A and CB-DC-1B are modeled as 480 V to charger supply breakers because their vertical feeders align with BUS-480-C and BUS-480-D but the long drop runs through a crowded boundary.
- SPARE-A2 is retained as a low-load spare node because DS-F015 terminates at a dashed spare box rather than a normal load symbol.
- Bottom relay and annunciator interlocks include medium and low confidence dash-dot or dashed edges where the rerouted lines visibly enter a named relay terminal or terminal-board bundle but share crowded corridors.
- BUS-120VAC-ESF to RPS or ESFAS panel power/control edges are retained as medium or low confidence mixed edges where bus taps and nearby panel wiring are visible but exact individual terminals are dense.
- Outer left and right vertical interlock corridors were not converted into broad bus-to-device edges unless a named relay panel terminal board or annunciator endpoint was visible.
- Plain wire crossings in the lower logic and top interlock corridors were not counted unless a filled junction dot or component terminal was visible.
