4) AMBIGUITIES_FOR_JENNIFER
- CHGR-125B appears fed by the right-side CABLE 1B-480-9 route from the 480 ESS B area. I represented this as BUS-480E1B to CHGR-125B but the long routed feeder is crowded near the right boundary.
- CHGR-125A supply is represented as BUS-480A to CHGR-125A based on the visible CABLE 1A-480-7 route from the left BUS-480A tap. The feeder is long and routed around the left boundary but visually reaches the charger.
- CB-105 to RELAY-K86A and CB-106 to RELAY-K86B are included as dashed interlock edges because the EDG breaker dashed routes are labeled CTRL-86A and CTRL-86B and continue to the 86 relay ports. The routes are long and partly boundary-adjacent.
- I did not add direct edges from RELAY-K86A or RELAY-K86B to JB-ESF-01 because the raw drawing shows the 86 relays routed to the RPS panels and the JB-ESF-01 dashed lines routed separately through the panels.
- I did not connect UPS-BUS-A to UPS-BUS-B or PANEL-RPS-A to PANEL-RPS-B. The central lower area has crossing enclosure and route lines but no clear terminal dot showing a bus tie.
- FDR A-DC FDR B-DC CABLE 1A-480-7 CABLE 1B-480-9 CTRL-86A CTRL-86B TB1 and terminal dots were treated as evidence labels or ports rather than component nodes.
- GND-1E is included only for the visible TR-1A grounding conductor. No matching explicit ground endpoint is drawn for TR-1B.
