Extraction overview
-------------------

This connectivity extraction is based on the provided RAW, component, wire, text, and junction/node layers for the synthetic nuclear plant one-line control power schematic.

To keep the adjacency matrix tractable while still reflecting the main electrical topology, I modeled:
- The major AC and DC buses.
- The main feeders from the 480 V safety buses to the intermediate safety/control buses.
- The control power transformers feeding the common control power bus.
- The 125 VDC inverter and DC distribution bus.
- The SSPS panel.
- Aggregated load groups for each safety train rather than every individual motor and protective device.

Component identifiers and naming
--------------------------------

- The original diagram uses detailed identifiers (e.g., RBC-HS-2A, RBC-VY-4A, RBC-BC-1ZB, RBC-DC-1ZB, specific breaker and transformer numbers). Many of these labels are very small on the overall sheet, so I adopted simplified, location-based identifiers such as BUS_HS2A, BUS_VY4A, BUS_BC1ZB, BUS_DC1ZB, etc.
- Tie breakers between the four top safety buses are modeled as TIE_AB, TIE_BC, and TIE_CD.
- The main feeders from each top bus down to the corresponding intermediate bus are modeled as FEED_A, FEED_B, FEED_C, and FEED_D.
- The control power transformers (and their associated protective devices) from each intermediate bus down to the common control power bus are modeled as CPT_A, CPT_B, CPT_C, and CPT_D.
- The transformer connecting the lower AC bus to the control power bus is modeled as XFORMER_HS1ZB_BC1ZB.
- The 125 VDC inverter and the DC distribution bus are modeled as INVERTER_A and BUS_DC1ZB.
- The SSPS panel is modeled simply as SSPS.
- All loads within each safety train fed from the common control power bus are aggregated into a single logical component per train: LOAD_TRAIN_A, LOAD_TRAIN_B, LOAD_TRAIN_C, and LOAD_TRAIN_D.

Ambiguities and assumptions
---------------------------

1. Text legibility:
   - Some component identifiers on the original sheet are too small to read with high confidence. Where the exact tag number was uncertain, I used simplified names based on bus function and physical location.
   - The simplified names will not match the exact plant tag numbers but preserve relative topology.

2. Component aggregation:
   - Each FEED_* component may in reality represent a breaker plus associated disconnects or fuses between a 480 V safety bus and the corresponding intermediate bus.
   - Each CPT_* component aggregates the primary and secondary disconnects, fuses, and transformer associated with feeding the control power bus from the respective intermediate bus.
   - XFORMER_HS1ZB_BC1ZB represents a transformer plus protective devices between the lower AC bus and the control power bus.
   - LOAD_TRAIN_* aggregates multiple motors and their individual starters, fuses, and contactors in that train. In the raw diagram, multiple motors are shown per train; I did not enumerate each motor separately.
   - Because of this aggregation, the matrix describes direct connectivity between these grouped components rather than every single device symbol.

3. SSPS connections:
   - The wire and junction layers show SSPS connected both to the DC distribution bus and to the control power bus. I modeled both direct connections (BUS_DC1ZB–SSPS and BUS_BC1ZB–SSPS).

4. Excluded detail:
   - Instrumentation, indicator lights, auxiliary contacts, and dashed control wiring are not represented in the matrix.
   - Any small internal interconnections within complex component symbols (for example, internal links within SSPS or within motor starter assemblies) are not modeled.
   - Any potential alternate or emergency feeds not clearly distinguishable from the main feeders were omitted to avoid speculative connections.

5. Junction vs. crossing:
   - The junction/node layer was used qualitatively to distinguish true junctions (solid dots) from simple graphical crossings. Only conductors with explicit junction nodes or continuous line segments were treated as electrical connections.

Validation-related notes
------------------------

- The matrix is square (29 x 29), symmetric, and the diagonal entries are all zero.
- Every edge in Jennifer_edges.csv corresponds to a pair of components marked with a "1" in Jennifer_matrix.csv.
- All connections in the matrix are supported by visible conductive paths in the wire layer and confirmed by junction nodes; I did not add connections solely to make the network appear more complete.
- The level of detail is intentionally coarse-grained (grouped feeders, transformers, and load groups) due to text legibility limitations and the complexity of the original one-line diagram.
